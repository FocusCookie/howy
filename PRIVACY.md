# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Howy is a menu-bar Eisenhower matrix for macOS. It doesn't collect, track or
sell any of your data. The developer can't see your todos.

## What Howy stores and where

- **On your Mac.** Your todos, notes, attachments, the daily backups and your
  settings are stored in Howy's own container on your Mac.
- **In your iCloud.** If you're signed in to iCloud, Howy syncs your todos and
  attachments to your **private** iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, so
  they show up on your other Macs. Only you can read this data. Apple handles
  it under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
  Without iCloud, Howy works on your Mac only.

## What Howy sends over the network

- **iCloud sync**, as described above.
- **Link titles.** When you paste a web link into a todo, Howy loads that page
  once to read its title. This request goes directly from your Mac to that
  website, as if you opened the link in a browser. Nothing is sent to the
  developer.

That's all. Howy has no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no tracking and
no accounts of its own.

## Deleting your data

Delete todos in the app, or delete Howy. To remove the synced copy, go to
**System Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage** and delete Howy's data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
